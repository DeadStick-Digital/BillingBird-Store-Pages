BillingBird

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Overview

BillingBird is designed as a local-first business tool. Core invoice, estimate, client, item, expense, receipt, mileage, per diem, payment, PDF, report, and archive records are stored on your device unless you choose to export, share, enable optional private iCloud backup, purchase a subscription, or contact support. The canonical DeadStick privacy policy is published at deadstick.net/privacy.html#apps.

Information You Create

BillingBird stores business names, client names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, invoice and estimate details, receipt images, expense details, mileage and per diem entries, manual payment records, tax-summary data, report exports, logo files, and local archive files that you enter or import into the app on your device. Unsaved invoice composer input and local financial recovery state are kept in protected local app storage on your device and are excluded from exported archives.

When Data Leaves Your Device

Data leaves your device only when you use features that require external transmission: explicit PDF, report, or archive export/sharing to a destination you choose; optional private iCloud backup and single-active-device handoff; BillingBird Pro subscription purchases, restores, and entitlement checks; or customer-initiated support messages.

Subscriptions (Apple App Store, Google Play & RevenueCat)

BillingBird Pro auto-renewable subscriptions are billed by Apple App Store or Google Play and validated through RevenueCat. Subscription processing uses purchase and entitlement history, product identifiers, subscription status, app-scoped subscriber identifiers, and vendor device identifiers (such as IDFV on iOS) for App Functionality (entitlement validation, purchase restoration, and subscription support) and subscription Analytics. BillingBird does not receive or store your full payment-card details, and does not use subscription data for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. See the BillingBird Subscription Terms.

Optional Private iCloud Backup & Third-Party Services

On iOS, if you explicitly enable iCloud Backup, BillingBird stores a versioned SQLite snapshot, app-owned attachment files, backup manifest metadata, and an active-device handoff lease in your personal private Apple CloudKit container (iCloud.com.billingbird.app). DeadStick does not operate a central cloud database of your readable invoices, clients, or receipts. Online invoice checkout is disabled in version 1.0.3; when optional connected payment providers (such as Stripe Connect) are enabled in a future release, payment processing will occur only when you explicitly connect a merchant account and create a checkout session.

Camera and Photos

BillingBird requests camera and photo-library access only for receipt scanning, receipt import, logo selection, and similar user-selected document workflows.

Security

BillingBird uses platform file protection and encrypted transport for network features where applicable. You are responsible for controlling access to your device, iCloud account, shared files, exported PDFs, and archive files.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local BillingBird records remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Optional private iCloud backups remain in your Apple iCloud account until you disable backup or delete the app's iCloud data. Subscription transaction records are retained by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat as needed for entitlement management, financial recordkeeping, dispute resolution, and legal obligations.

For local, iCloud, and support data deletion steps, visit BillingBird Data Deletion or deadstick.net/deletion.html.

Children

BillingBird is intended for business use and is not directed to children.

Contact

For privacy questions, email contact@deadstick.net.
