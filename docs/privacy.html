BillingBird

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

Overview

BillingBird is designed as a local-first business tool. Core invoice, estimate, client, item, expense, receipt, payment, PDF, report, and archive records are stored on your device unless you choose to export, share, back up, sync, publish, or contact support.

Information You Create

BillingBird may store business names, client names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, invoice and estimate details, receipt images, expense details, payment records, tax-summary data, report exports, logo files, and local archive files that you enter or import into the app.

When Data Leaves Your Device

Data can leave your device when you use optional features such as PDF sharing, email sharing, archive export/import, receipt image import, iCloud Drive backup, Google Drive backup where available, BillingBird Cloud features, hosted invoices, hosted estimates, online payments, app-store subscriptions, or support email.

Subscriptions

BillingBird Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat. Those services process purchase, renewal, cancellation, restore, and entitlement information so BillingBird can confirm paid access. BillingBird does not store full payment-card details.

Cloud, Payments, and Third-Party Services

If you choose online or cloud-connected features, BillingBird may transmit relevant account, invoice, estimate, payment, workspace, and support information to service providers used to operate those features. These providers may include Apple, Google, RevenueCat, Supabase, UNIPaaS, and email or support systems selected by the user.

Camera and Photos

BillingBird requests camera and photo-library access only for receipt scanning, receipt import, logo selection, and similar user-selected document workflows.

Security

BillingBird uses platform storage and encrypted transport for network features where applicable. You are responsible for controlling access to your device, backups, shared files, exported PDFs, and archive files.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local BillingBird records remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Exported, shared, backed-up, or cloud-published records may also exist wherever you sent them. To request help with support records or cloud-related data, contact BillingBird support.

For cloud account deletion steps and support contact options, visit BillingBird Account and Data Deletion.

Children

BillingBird is intended for business use and is not directed to children.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@deadstickdigital.com.
