BillingBird

Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: June 27, 2026

Delete a BillingBird Cloud Account

BillingBird is local-first, so you can use core invoice and receipt features without a BillingBird Cloud account. If you created a cloud account, you can delete it in the app from Settings, Cloud Account, Delete Cloud Account.

The app asks you to review the warning, type DELETE, and confirm before deletion. Successful deletion removes the cloud account, owned BillingBird Cloud workspaces, hosted invoice and estimate links, local BillingBird app data, and the local cloud session on that device.

Request Help

If you cannot access the app or need help with cloud-related or support records, email support@deadstickdigital.com with the subject line “BillingBird deletion request”.

Do not include sensitive client, payment, tax, or business records unless support specifically asks for them.

Local Files, Backups, and Subscriptions

Local files, exported PDFs, shared archives, device backups, iCloud Drive backups, or Google Drive backups may remain wherever you saved or sent them. Delete those copies from the device or service where they are stored.

Deleting a BillingBird Cloud account does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Manage subscriptions from BillingBird Pro in the app, your Apple ID subscription settings, or your Google Play account settings.
