Support

Last updated: June 13, 2026

Get Help

For BillingBird support, email support@deadstickdigital.com.

What To Include

Include your device model, operating system version, BillingBird app version, the feature you were using, and a short description of what happened. Do not send sensitive client, payment, tax, or business records unless support specifically needs them.

Subscriptions

BillingBird Pro subscriptions are managed by Apple App Store or Google Play through RevenueCat. You can restore purchases or open subscription management from the BillingBird Pro screen in the app. You can also manage or cancel subscriptions from your Apple ID or Google Play account settings.

Local Data

BillingBird is local-first. If you delete the app or device data without a backup or export, local invoices, receipts, PDFs, reports, and archive files may be lost.

Account and Data Deletion

If you created a BillingBird Cloud account, you can delete it in the app from Settings, Cloud Account, Delete Cloud Account. Read the Account and Data Deletion page for the full deletion path and support contact option.

Privacy

Read the BillingBird Privacy Policy for information about local storage, optional sharing, cloud features, support email, and subscription processing.
