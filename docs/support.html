Support

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Get Help

For BillingBird support, email contact@deadstick.net or visit deadstick.net/support.html.

What To Include

Include your device model, operating system version, BillingBird app version, the feature you were using, and a short description of what happened. Do not send sensitive client, payment, tax, or business records unless support specifically needs them.

Subscriptions

BillingBird Pro subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID or Google Play account with RevenueCat entitlement support. You can restore purchases, open the Customer Center, or manage your subscription from Settings → Subscription inside the app, or in your device's Apple ID / Google Play Subscriptions settings.

Local Data & Private iCloud Backup

BillingBird is local-first. If you delete the app or device data without exporting an archive or enabling optional private iCloud backup on iOS, local invoices, receipts, PDFs, reports, and archive files on that device will be removed.

Data Deletion

BillingBird does not require a DeadStick cloud account. Read the Data Deletion page for instructions on deleting local records, private iCloud backups, and support or subscription metadata.

Privacy & Terms

Read the BillingBird Privacy Policy, the full DeadStick App Privacy Policy, and the BillingBird Subscription Terms.
